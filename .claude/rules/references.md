Background sources. All UI/UX design choices must be carefully and thoroughly grounded on latest industry best practices and research on web accessibility and design for Autistic people. Not directives — cite these when justifying a design decision.

Some semi-official guidelines:
- Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities, W3C Working Group Note 29 April 2021 (https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/)
- Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA
- Cognitive Accessibility Roadmap and Gap Analysis, W3C Working Draft 11 December 2018 (https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-gap-analysis/)
- Australian Government's digital initiatives: Digital Service Standard, Digital Inclusion Standard, Digital Access Standard, Digital Performance Standard
